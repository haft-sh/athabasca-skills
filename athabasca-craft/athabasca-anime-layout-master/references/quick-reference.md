# Anime Layout Master Quick Reference

## Default operating order
1. Emotional and narrative reading
2. Composition
3. Camera
4. Perspective
5. Focus
6. Depth
7. Continuity / blocking / editability
8. Reading issues
9. Concrete improvements

## What a strong shot needs
- A dominant emotion
- A clear first read
- A meaningful camera relationship to the subject
- A readable silhouette
- Depth layers
- Controlled visual hierarchy
- A reason for the chosen horizon
- A reason for the chosen vanishing point
- A clear axis and screen direction when the shot belongs to a sequence
- A plausible cut in and cut out

## Variant labels
- More dramatic
- More naturalistic
- More cinematic

## Prompt checklist
Include:
- framing
- camera
- horizon
- vanishing point
- depth layers
- visual focus
- emotion
- lighting
- direction of movement
- silhouette

For sequences also include:
- axis / line of action
- screen direction
- eyeline
- blocking state
- continuity guards
- cut point

## Sequence mini-checklist
- Which shot establishes geography?
- Where is the axis?
- Which shot carries the emotional turn?
- Which shot functions as the reaction?
- Is an insert or cutaway needed?
- Which continuity lock cannot be broken?

## Common shorthand
- Size: `ECU`, `CU`, `MCU`, `MS`, `FS`, `WS`, `EWS`
- Type: `OTS`, `POV`, `2S`, `INS`, `C/A`, `EST`
- Angle: `EL`, `LA`, `HA`, `DUTCH`, `TOP`, `NADIR`
- Movement: `PAN`, `TILT`, `DOLLY IN`, `TRUCK`, `ARC`, `RACK FOCUS`, `LOCKOFF`
- Continuity: `AXIS HOLD`, `CROSS LINE`, `RESET AXIS`, `DIR: L→R`, `MATCH ACT`, `HARD CUT`
