# Educational science explainer script notes

Use when converting a research synthesis into a short educational animated explainer script.

## Structure that worked

- Cold open: concrete conflict or misconception.
- Establish taxonomy/context in one short beat.
- Explain the evolutionary/causal sequence as linked steps, not as a list of facts.
- Insert “did you know” facts where they support the sequence rather than as trivia dumps.
- Close by restating the mechanism: multiple small advantages compound into an integrated lifestyle.

## Accuracy habits

- Avoid teleology: do not imply animals evolved traits “in order to” achieve a planned final form. Phrase as selection favoring variants that improved survival/reproduction.
- Avoid direct-ancestor claims unless the literature is explicit. Use “relative,” “lineage,” “stem/sister taxon,” or “shows the trait existed by this time.”
- Separate consensus from hypotheses with phrases like “one current hypothesis…” or “the evidence suggests…”
- For student audiences, simplify vocabulary but keep the causal logic accurate.
- Keep bibliography and deeper detail in the research report; keep the VO script light.

## Example beat formula

```text
VISUAL: [specific animated action]
VO: [plain-language claim]
ON SCREEN: [short label or date]
ACCURACY NOTE: [optional note for production, not spoken]
```

## Useful “did you know” integration pattern

For anatomy/behavior facts, connect the fact to the survival system:

- Teeth: self-sharpening incisors → harvest woody food/material.
- Lips behind incisors: underwater gnawing/carrying without swallowing water.
- Hind feet: webbed propulsion; forefeet: manipulation and grooming.
- Fur: dense underfur + guard hairs + oils → cold-water insulation.
- Nictitating membranes, closable ears/nostrils: underwater work package.
- Tail: swimming/balance/warning/thermoregulation/fat storage, not just “paddle.”
- Social colony: maintenance workforce for infrastructure-heavy behavior.

## Persistence note

If research and script are both produced in the same session, write them as separate phase reports: `phase: "research"` for the bibliography/synthesis and `phase: "script"` for the production draft.
